## 01. /about

I am drawn to the invisible parts of a good experience: the rules beneath a game mechanic, the architecture behind a smooth interface, and the small decisions that make software feel deliberate instead of accidental.

My lane sits between engineering and play. That can mean shipping a focused web tool, tracing a bug down to first principles, prototyping an interaction, or tuning feedback until an idea becomes something you can feel. I care about structure, polish, and projects with a point of view.

> **Working principle** — make it work, understand why it works, then make it feel right.
